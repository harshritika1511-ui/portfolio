Case Study · Careers360 · Jul 2024 – Present

Polls &
Discussions

A complete UGC content creation and distribution system that generated 300,000+ votes and doubled platform engagement — built lean enough to run without ongoing editorial effort.

The Problem

Careers360 had strong informational content but pages were largely static, resulting in low dwell time, minimal return visits, and weak signals for search engine indexing.

Manually creating and mapping polls to the right pages at the right time was effort-intensive, making it difficult to scale across hundreds of exam, college, and result pages simultaneously.

My Role

As Product Manager, I owned end-to-end delivery — from identifying the opportunity through user research and competitive analysis, to defining requirements for the poll CMS, AI suggestion engine, auto-scroll behaviour, and entity mapping logic, through to working with engineering and design on execution and monitoring post-launch KPIs.

Key Insights

User Research

Students actively sought peer validation for exam and college decisions on external platforms — confirming latent demand for opinion tools Careers360 could own natively.

Platform Behaviour

Pages with interactive elements had significantly higher dwell time and lower bounce rates than static pages — polls presented a low-friction path to engagement.

Operational Reality

Manual scaling would require unsustainable editorial bandwidth. Building an AI suggestion engine was the only path to quality at scale.

Search Signal

UGC — especially comments tied to specific exams and colleges — generates long-tail keywords that improve crawl frequency and ranking.

Product Architecture

01

Poll Creation CMS

A dedicated internal CMS for the editorial team to create, schedule, and publish polls without engineering involvement — with configurable questions, answer options, target entities, and display rules.

02

AI Poll Suggester

An AI-powered suggestion engine integrated into the CMS. Based on the target entity (exam, college, article), it suggested contextually relevant questions and answer options — reducing creation time from hours to minutes.

03

Auto-Scroll & Sequential Discovery

Polls auto-scrolled to the next in sequence without manual interaction, extending session depth and increasing total votes per visit by mimicking familiar social-scroll behaviour.

04

Keyword & Entity Mapping

Each poll was mapped to entities and keywords, enabling automatic surfacing on relevant pages. A fallback keyword-based auto-fetch ensured no page was ever left without an active poll — making distribution self-sustaining.

Outcomes

Total votes 3,00,000+ in 2 months
Comments generated 30,000+
Platform engagement ↑ 100%
Page performance Improved dwell time, return visits, session depth
Search visibility Improved indexing via continuous UGC
Operational efficiency Poll creation time cut from hours to minutes
Platform coverage 100% via auto-fetch, no manual placement
Lead quality impact Higher engagement → improved registrations

The Problem

Teams lacked a centralised view of website health and performance. Current methods were fragmented across tools, resulting in inefficiencies in identifying SEO, content, server, or API-related issues.

There was no monitoring and alert system to reduce reaction time when issues arose — teams were reactive, not proactive, and had no direct path from detection to ticket creation.

Solution

The Website Performance Monitor is designed as a centralised hub for real-time analysis — enabling internal teams to proactively identify improvement areas and receive instant alerts for anomalies across four pillars: Server Performance, SEO Performance, API Performance, and Page Metrics.

Its key differentiator is the combination of monitoring + alerting + direct ticket-raising, eliminating the mediator step between detection and resolution.

Target Audiences

SEO Team Track indexing, crawling, user activity, and domain-level traffic
Content Team Monitor content compliance and detect guideline violations
Technology Team Assess site speed, server health, and API response times
Product Team Measure engagement, prioritise fixes, monitor traffic trends
Management Consolidated strategic oversight and cross-functional insights
QA Team Identify response time issues for quick bug reporting

Key Features

01

Server Status Dashboard

At-a-glance operational status of all servers (Online, Offline, Errors). First section users see on access — instant infrastructure health for technical teams.

02

SEO Analytics Section

Visualises Active Users, Total Views, Total Users, Average Engagement plus real-time graphs for Crawl Requests and Page Response Times.

03

Page Performance Section

Published pages count, response times, and unavailability data — presented in block format for quick identification of underperforming pages.

04

Pages Overview + Insights

SEO ratings per page with drill-down via Analyze. Actions: Visit page, auto-raise Jira ticket, or deep-dive into the Page Insights section.

05

API Analytics Section

Most recent API hits across all domains with dynamic graphs visualising frequency and trends — enabling backend teams to spot anomalies and traffic spikes.

06

Alert System

Auto-email/Slack alerts for anomalies and guideline breaches with threshold-based triggers (e.g., index ratio drop, crawl anomaly, traffic dip).
